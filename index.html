          This is wellbeing guidance, not medical care. Speak to a clinician if symptoms affect daily life, your cycle changes suddenly, you have bleeding between periods, or you may be pregnant.

          Seek urgent medical help for severe or sudden pain, very heavy bleeding with dizziness or fainting, or possible pregnancy with pain or bleeding.

          Hormonal contraception, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, perimenopause and conditions such as PCOS or endometriosis can make phase estimates less useful. Use this primarily as a symptom journal unless a clinician has advised otherwise.
